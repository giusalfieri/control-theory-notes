# Slides

Visual study slides on single topics of control theory. Each deck follows one or more
video lectures, credited on its title slide.

| Deck | Contents | PDF | Source videos |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lyapunov stability and Lyapunov functions** | trajectories, motions and equilibria; stability, attractivity, asymptotic stability; the pendulum energy; Lyapunov's direct method with a step-by-step graphical proof; why Lyapunov functions are needed beyond energy | [lyapunov.pdf](lyapunov/lyapunov.pdf) (55 slides) | [A Visual Explanation of Lyapunov Stability](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W8YpgG0KuOo), [A Visual Explanation of Lyapunov Functions](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dpZnYiGWLT0) |
| **The PID controller** | open loop and feedback; bang-bang and proportional control; steady-state error; integral and derivative action; tuning the three gains | [pid.pdf](pid/pid.pdf) (32 slides) | [The Most Important Algorithm You've Never Heard Of](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9gZojywQPBY) |
